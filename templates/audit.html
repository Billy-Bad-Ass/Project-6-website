Questions

Before you buy

Is this just an automated scan?

No. Automated tools are part of the process — they are good at measuring things — but the report is written by a person who looked at your site. A scanner cannot tell you that your pricing page does not say what you sell.

What if my site is already fine?

Then the report says so, names the few things worth doing anyway, and you get your money back. Charging $100 to say "looks good" is not a business worth running.

Do you fix the problems too?

Not as part of this. The report is written so your developer can act on it directly. If you would rather it were done for you, reply to the report and we can talk about it.

How fast is "one working day"?

Ordered on a Tuesday morning, you have it by Wednesday. Ordered on a Friday evening, you have it by Monday.
